# Beacon Biosignals

> Beacon Biosignals develops AI-powered neurotechnology for brain health, applying machine learning to EEG data at scale for precision-neuroscience drug development and home sleep testing diagnostics. Beacon does not publish a public developer API; this profile centers on its open-source Julia software and company identity.
